PhotoFeather

Photo too large for a form or email?

Choose a file-size target or resize setting, compare the measured result, then save a new JPEG copy. Your originals stay untouched. All processing is on your device, with no account, ads or purchases. JPEG output is lossy; quality and savings vary, and some targets cannot be met.

ShareSafe

Check what travels with your photo.

ShareSafe creates new JPEG copies with embedded metadata removed. Cleaning and selected EXIF checks are free; Pro editing tools are optional. Photo processing stays on-device. Visible faces, addresses and text still need your review. The Play edition includes ML Kit diagnostics; photos are not uploaded to the developer.

PaperBuild

A few page photos. One PDF.

Select photos, crop and arrange the pages, then export a PDF on your Android phone. The free version supports up to 10 pages; optional one-time Pro adds more tools. Documents stay on-device, with no account or ads.

Northlight

A clear compass heading, even offline.

Northlight combines a magnetic compass, spoken headings and practical light tools. Core features are free, with optional one-time Pro tools. Compass accuracy depends on your phone and nearby interference; it is not route navigation. No account or ads.

Kreativ Fancy Text

Make the words look like you.

Style text to copy and paste, or export matching PNG artwork. Create on your Android phone, offline, with no account, ads or purchases. Copied text depends on the receiving app; use PNG to preserve the artwork. This does not change your phone's system font.

Kreativ Font

Build a better font shortlist.

Browse the Kreativ Font catalogue, inspect previews and keep favorites on your Android phone. This is a font discovery app, not a phone-font installer. Browsing needs internet; linked fonts have their own prices and licences.
