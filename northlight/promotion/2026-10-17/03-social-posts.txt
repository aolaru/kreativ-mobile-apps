Community post where developer promotion is permitted

Developer disclosure: I made Northlight, a small Android compass and light utility.

The free tools include a magnetic compass with calibration feedback, optional spoken headings, a GPS altimeter showing its reference and uncertainty, an LED lantern, red screen light and SOS flashing signal. There are no ads or account.

Optional one-time Pro adds saved magnetic bearings, spoken phone-turn guidance and extra light controls. Saved bearings are directions, not destinations; the app does not plan routes or make emergency calls.

I would appreciate feedback on the compass readability and whether the altitude uncertainty is understandable. No ratings, positive reviews or purchases requested.
