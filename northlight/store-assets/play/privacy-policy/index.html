Northlight Privacy Policy

Unpublished draft for the next Northlight release · prepared 15 October 2026

Northlight is a compass and lantern app made by Kreativ. Its compass and light tools are designed to work on your device.

Data we collect

Kreativ does not collect, sell, or share personal data through Northlight. The app has no account system, analytics, advertising SDKs, or developer-operated cloud services. Saved bearings, light-brightness preferences, speech speed and interval, the optional alignment-haptic preference, altitude units and whether a location permission request has been made, a cached Pro entitlement, and pending purchase-confirmation tokens are stored only on your device. Northlight does not save coordinates, routes, altitude history or audio recordings. These app records are excluded from Android cloud backup and device transfer.

Optional Northlight Pro purchase

Northlight Pro is an optional one-time purchase processed by Google Play. The app uses internet access to communicate with Google Play Billing when it opens, when you buy or restore Pro, and when it retries purchase confirmation in the background. This checks ownership and confirms completed purchases; compass and light tools remain usable offline. Google handles payment under its own privacy policy; Northlight does not receive or store payment-card details. Pending confirmation tokens are held locally by Android WorkManager for retries, and its completed-job records are subsequently pruned.

Device permissions

Northlight requests camera permission only when you use its LED lantern, SOS signal, or Pro strobe. Android requires this permission to control the camera flash. Northlight never takes photos, records video, or accesses your media.

The compass uses your device's orientation sensors locally to show magnetic north. Sensor readings do not leave your device.

Optional GPS altimeter

The free altimeter requests precise foreground location only after you choose to enable it. Android supplies a GPS/GNSS location fix; Northlight uses its altitude, vertical uncertainty, timing and simulated-location flag to display a current altitude estimate. Northlight does not save the fix or coordinates, build a route, or send location or altitude to Kreativ or an elevation server. Only the current altitude estimate is held temporarily in memory. GPS measurement stops when you stop it, leave the home screen or lock the phone, and does not restart automatically. The app does not request background location. Compass, light and spoken-compass features work without location access. Android's own location services and device settings govern how the operating system obtains satellite fixes.

Optional spoken compass

Northlight sends a direction or accuracy warning to an installed Android text-to-speech engine using a voice marked as offline and installed, in the app's language. It does not send headings to a developer-operated speech service, use the microphone, or record audio. Speech stops when you leave the app. If a screen reader is active, an explicitly requested reading is handed to that accessibility service instead, which uses its own voice, connectivity and privacy settings. Choosing to manage or download voices opens external Android/voice-engine settings; voice downloads may use the internet and are governed by that provider.

Pro owners can optionally request spoken phone-turn/alignment instructions for a saved magnetic bearing, with optional native alignment haptic feedback. This uses the same local speech path, not GPS or a routing service. Saved bearing names are not spoken; prompts use direction and degrees. Guidance pauses when you leave its screen or change/clear the target and never restarts automatically. The haptic preference is stored locally, and feedback respects Android settings and hardware availability. These cues describe phone orientation, not obstacle avoidance, safe routes or arrival at a place.

Children

Northlight does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.
