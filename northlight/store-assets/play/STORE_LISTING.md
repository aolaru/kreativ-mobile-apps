## Full description

Northlight puts a clear compass, optional spoken headings, GPS altitude and essential light tools in one calm, private app.

Find your bearing with a large magnetic compass and readable degree markings. Request a spoken heading, check an approximate GPS altitude, turn on the LED lantern, use a red screen light in low-light conditions, or flash an SOS signal when you need attention.

FREE TOOLS

- Offline compass with magnetic-north heading and direction inside the dial
- Accuracy feedback and simple figure-eight calibration guidance
- Optional spoken compass: hear one heading or enable automatic announcements
- Installed offline English or Romanian speech; screen-reader handoff when active
- Optional GPS/GNSS altimeter with metres or feet, altitude reference and reported vertical accuracy when available
- LED lantern with adjustable brightness on supported phones
- Red screen light with adjustable brightness
- SOS flashing signal
- Quick Settings tile for fast torch access
- No account, ads, analytics or tracking

NORTHLIGHT PRO — OPTIONAL ONE-TIME PURCHASE

- Save up to eight named magnetic bearings on this device; follow, rename or delete them
- Open saved bearings directly from the home screen
- Optional spoken turn/alignment guidance and alignment haptic feedback for a selected bearing
- Amber and green screen lights, plus an adjustable flash strobe
- Restore a purchase using the same Google Play account; no subscription

Compass, spoken headings, GPS altitude, lantern, red light and SOS remain free. Screen lights stay awake while open. Torch brightness and haptic feedback depend on phone hardware and Android settings.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

Northlight has no account, advertising, analytics or tracking. Camera access is requested only for flash-based tools; the app never takes photos or records video. Precise foreground location is optional and requested only for the GPS altimeter. Coordinates, routes and altitude history are not saved or uploaded. Speech uses an installed offline voice; the app does not use the microphone or record audio. Managing or downloading voices may require an internet connection. A screen reader uses its own voice and privacy settings.

Compass and light tools work offline. GPS altitude needs usable satellite reception; mobile data is not required, but the first fix may take time. Google Play needs internet access for purchase, restore and ownership confirmation. Website links open your browser only when tapped.

IMPORTANT LIMITATIONS

The compass points to magnetic north, not true north. Metal, magnets and phone cases can affect it. GPS altitude is an estimate: its reference and reported vertical uncertainty are shown, and WGS84 height is not sea level or height above ground. Spoken and saved-bearing cues describe phone orientation, not walking direction, obstacle avoidance, safe routes or arrival. Northlight is not a substitute for emergency communications or safety-critical navigation. Use flashing lights with care around people sensitive to flashes.
