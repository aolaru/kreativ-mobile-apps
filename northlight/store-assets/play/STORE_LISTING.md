## Full description

Northlight puts calm, essential outdoor tools in one private, offline app.

Find your bearing with a clear magnetic compass, light your way with a bright LED lantern, preserve night vision with a red-light mode, or send an SOS flash signal when you need attention.

Built for the quiet moments:

- Offline compass with magnetic-north heading
- Accuracy feedback and simple figure-eight calibration guidance
- LED lantern with adjustable brightness on supported phones
- Red-light screen mode for low-light use
- SOS flashing signal
- Quick Settings tile for fast torch access
- No account, ads, analytics or internet permission

Northlight uses camera access only when you choose a flash-based lantern or SOS feature. It never takes photos or records video.
