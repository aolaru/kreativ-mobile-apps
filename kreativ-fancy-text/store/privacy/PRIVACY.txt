Kreativ Fancy Text — Privacy Policy
Effective date: 4 November 2026 (1.1.0 Pro update)
Android application: com.kreativfont.studio
Developer: Kreativ
Contact: info@madebykreativ.com

This policy describes the Kreativ Fancy Text Android app, whose launcher name
is Kreativ. It does not describe Kreativ websites, the WordPress plugin, or the
Chrome extension.

Processing on your device
Your text, signature, styling choices, and PNG artwork are processed on your
device. Fonts and artwork are bundled with the app. The app has no account,
advertising, analytics, crash-reporting service, remote rendering, or internet
permission. It does not transmit your text, designs, identifiers, or usage data
to Kreativ or to a server operated on our behalf.

Drafts, favorites, presets, and retention
Your current draft, up to 50 favorite designs, and up to 30 named style presets
are saved in the app's local preferences until you edit or remove them, clear
the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app. Android cloud backup is disabled. Clear removes
the text from the current draft; delete each saved design separately to remove
favorites. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes all app-local designs.

Clipboard, files, and sharing
Copy text writes only the output you choose to the system clipboard. Clipboard
availability and retention are controlled by Android and your device settings.
Save PNG writes an image to the location you choose in Android's system file
picker. The app does not request broad access to your photos or files.
Share text and Share image pass only your chosen output to Android's share
sheet and the receiving app you select. These are actions you initiate. The
receiving app or storage provider handles that output under its own practices.
Exported or shared copies are outside the app's saved-design storage and are
not removed when you delete a favorite or uninstall Kreativ. You can delete
those copies in the app or location where you saved or shared them.

Optional Pro purchase
Fancy Text Pro is an optional, one-time purchase processed by Google Play. The app
requests product prices, starts purchases you choose, and checks or restores
your purchase through the Google Play Billing Library. Google handles payment
details under its own privacy policy; Kreativ does not receive your card details.
Your text and artwork are not included in these billing requests.

A signed purchase record and signature are stored in private app preferences
to recognise Pro access offline. Completed purchases are acknowledged through
Google Play; Android may retry this acknowledgement after an interruption.
When Google Play confirms that a purchase is no longer owned, cached access is
removed. Clearing app storage removes this local record; Restore purchase checks
the current Google Play account. No purchase record is sent to a Kreativ server.

Temporary image files
Images prepared for saving and sharing use private app cache folders. Temporary
save files are removed after completion or cancellation. Older files in an
export folder are cleaned on its next use after 24 hours. Android may also
clear cache files. Uninstalling or clearing app storage removes this cache.

Permissions and third-party components
The app does not access contacts, location, camera, microphone, device-wide
photos, or advertising identifiers. It does not install a keyboard or use an
accessibility service. Capacitor components provide local storage, clipboard,
file, and share functionality; fonts are included locally. Google Play Billing
provides optional payments and purchase restoration. AndroidX WorkManager retries
purchase acknowledgements; its network-state, wake-lock and boot-completed
permissions support that work. There is no foreground service or background
upload of designs. Google Play handles its own network connection; the app does
not request the Android internet permission. No third-party
advertising, analytics, or remote font service is integrated.

Support correspondence
If you choose to email support, we receive your email address and the contents
you send so that we can respond. Do not send sensitive designs or personal
information that is unnecessary to your question. Support mail is separate
from the app; we do not automatically attach app data. You may contact us about
that correspondence or request deletion at info@madebykreativ.com, subject to
any records we must retain to meet applicable obligations.

Changes and contact
We will update this policy and its effective date if the app's practices change.
For privacy questions, contact info@madebykreativ.com.
