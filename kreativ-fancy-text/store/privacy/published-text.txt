This policy describes the Kreativ Fancy Text Android app, whose launcher name is Kreativ. It does not describe Kreativ websites, the WordPress plugin, or the Chrome extension.

Processing on your device

Your text, signature, styling choices, and PNG artwork are processed on your device. Fonts and artwork are bundled with the app. The app has no account, advertising, analytics, crash-reporting service, remote rendering, or internet permission. It does not transmit your text, designs, identifiers, or usage data to Kreativ or to a server operated on our behalf.

Drafts, favorites, and retention

Your current draft and up to 50 favorite designs are saved in the app's local preferences until you edit or remove them, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app. Android cloud backup is disabled. Clear removes the text from the current draft; delete each saved design separately to remove favorites. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes all app-local designs.

Clipboard, files, and sharing

Copy text writes only the output you choose to the system clipboard. Clipboard availability and retention are controlled by Android and your device settings. Save PNG writes an image to the location you choose in Android's system file picker. The app does not request broad access to your photos or files. Share text and Share image pass only your chosen output to Android's share sheet and the receiving app you select. These are actions you initiate. The receiving app or storage provider handles that output under its own practices. Exported or shared copies are outside the app's saved-design storage and are not removed when you delete a favorite or uninstall Kreativ. You can delete those copies in the app or location where you saved or shared them.

Temporary image files

Images prepared for saving and sharing use private app cache folders. Temporary save files are removed after completion or cancellation. Older files in an export folder are cleaned on its next use after 24 hours. Android may also clear cache files. Uninstalling or clearing app storage removes this cache.

Permissions and third-party components

The app does not access contacts, location, camera, microphone, device-wide photos, or advertising identifiers. It does not install a keyboard or use an accessibility service. Capacitor components provide local storage, clipboard, file, and share functionality; fonts are included locally. No third-party advertising, analytics, or remote font service is integrated.

Support correspondence

If you choose to email support, we receive your email address and the contents you send so that we can respond. Do not send sensitive designs or personal information that is unnecessary to your question. Support mail is separate from the app; we do not automatically attach app data. You may contact us about that correspondence or request deletion at info@madebykreativ.com, subject to any records we must retain to meet applicable obligations.

Changes and contact

We will update this policy and its effective date if the app's practices change. For privacy questions, contact info@madebykreativ.com.
