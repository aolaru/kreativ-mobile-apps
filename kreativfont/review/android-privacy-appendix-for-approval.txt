Kreativ Font Android app privacy
Last updated: 11 October 2026

This section applies to the Kreativ Font Android app (com.kreativ.fontapp),
version 2.2.1. The app is provided by Andrei Olaru / Kreativ Font. The website's
membership, AI-tool and advertising features are not native app features.

The app displays recent commercial and free font articles, descriptions and
preview images from kreativfont.com. It does not require an account, contain
advertising or analytics SDKs, or request access to your camera, photos,
contacts or device location. It does not run the website's Google Analytics,
Jetpack Stats or advertising JavaScript inside the native app.

Data stored on your device

Favorites, cached article information and preview images are stored in the
app's private storage on your device. In-app search filters the loaded articles
locally. The native app does not upload your favorites or in-app search terms.
You can remove favorites individually. Clearing the app's storage through
Android settings or uninstalling it removes its local data. Android cloud
backup and device transfer are disabled for the app.

Catalogue requests and hosting logs

To load current articles and preview images, the app sends HTTPS requests to
kreativfont.com and WordPress/Jetpack image CDN servers operated by Automattic.
These services receive your IP address and technical request information,
such as the requested URL, request time and user-agent information supplied
by your device. They process this information to deliver content, operate and
protect the service, and investigate technical problems.

WordPress.com's documentation states that its web-server logs record server
requests and are retained for 30 days. Automattic's visitor privacy notice
describes keeping IP-address, browser and operating-system information in
web-server logs for approximately 30 days, with retention subject to legal
requirements. This is separate from the app's local storage.

Our website also uses the Redirection plugin. Requests that are redirected
or encounter a missing page/file may be logged locally on the website with
their full IP address. Redirect and missing-page logs are configured to expire
after one day; cleanup is performed by WordPress's scheduled tasks. Additional
HTTP-header capture is disabled.

See Automattic's visitor privacy notice:
https://automattic.com/privacy-notice/
WordPress.com server-log documentation:
https://wordpress.com/support/site-logs/

Opening websites

Article links and the privacy policy open in your browser, outside the native
app. These visits are governed by the website's privacy, cookie, analytics and
affiliate disclosures, together with your browser's privacy settings. Older
migrated favorites without an article link may open a website search; the
browser then sends that search query to the website. Other linked websites
have their own privacy policies.

Contact and data requests

For privacy questions or requests concerning data associated with the app's
network requests, contact contact@kreativfont.com. We will assess requests
with the relevant hosting provider where necessary. Retention required for
security or legal obligations may limit deletion. Do not send passwords or
other sensitive information with your request. We cannot retrieve favorites
or search terms that are stored only on your device.
