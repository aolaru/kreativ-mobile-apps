Suggested replacement for the privacy appendix's "Data stored on your device"
paragraph (DRAFT; NOT PUBLISHED):

Favorites, cached article information and preview images are stored in the app's
private storage on your device. Searching your saved favorites filters them locally.
The native app does not upload favorites or persist search phrases. You can remove
favorites individually. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes local data.
Android cloud backup and device transfer are disabled for the app.

Suggested new paragraph under "Catalogue requests and hosting logs"
(DRAFT; NOT PUBLISHED):

Searching the website catalogue from the app sends your search phrase to
kreativfont.com in an HTTPS request so that the website can return matching font
articles. Search phrases are part of the requested URL and may therefore appear
in the hosting logs described below. An internet connection is required. Avoid
entering personal or sensitive information in font searches. This is separate
from the local search of saved favorites.
