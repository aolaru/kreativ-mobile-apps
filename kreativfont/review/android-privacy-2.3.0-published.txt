This section applies to the Kreativ Font Android app (com.kreativ.fontapp),
versions 2.2.1 and 2.3.0. Online catalogue search described below is available
from version 2.3.0; version 2.2.1 searches loaded articles locally. The app is
provided by Andrei Olaru / Kreativ Font. The website's membership, AI-tool and
advertising features are not native app features.

The app displays recent commercial and free font articles, descriptions and
preview images from kreativfont.com. It does not require an account, contain
advertising or analytics SDKs, or request access to your camera, photos,
contacts or device location. It does not run the website's Google Analytics,
Jetpack Stats or advertising JavaScript inside the native app.

Data stored on your device

Favorites, cached article information and preview images are stored in the
app's private storage on your device. Searching saved favorites filters them
locally; favorites are not uploaded by the native app. The app does not
maintain a search-history list. Android may retain the current query
temporarily to restore the search screen. You can remove favorites
individually. Clearing the app's storage through Android settings or
uninstalling it removes its local data. Android cloud backup and device
transfer are disabled for the app.

Catalogue requests and hosting logs

In version 2.3.0, searching the website catalogue sends your search phrase to
kreativfont.com in an HTTPS request so the website can return matching font
articles. The phrase is included in the requested URL and may therefore
appear in the hosting logs described below. Catalogue search is optional:
you can browse font sections and use favorites without entering a search.
An internet connection is required for catalogue search. Avoid entering
personal or sensitive information in font searches. This is separate from
the local search of saved favorites.

To load current articles and preview images, the app sends HTTPS requests to
kreativfont.com and WordPress/Jetpack image CDN servers operated by Automattic.
These services receive your IP address and technical request information,
such as the requested URL, request time and user-agent information supplied
by your device. They process this information to deliver content, operate
and protect the service, and investigate technical problems.

WordPress.com's documentation states that its web-server logs record server
requests and are retained for 30 days. Automattic's visitor privacy notice
describes keeping IP-address, browser and operating-system information in
web-server logs for approximately 30 days, with retention subject to legal
requirements. This is separate from the app's local storage.

Our website also uses the Redirection plugin. Requests that are redirected
or encounter a missing page/file may be logged locally on the website with
their full IP address. Redirect and missing-page logs are configured to
expire after one day; cleanup is performed by WordPress's scheduled tasks.
Additional HTTP-header capture is disabled.

See Automattic's visitor privacy notice:
https://automattic.com/privacy-notice/
WordPress.com server-log documentation:
https://wordpress.com/support/site-logs/

Opening websites

Article links, the privacy policy and the Made by Kreativ credit link to
madebykreativ.com/apps open in your browser, outside the native app. These
visits are governed by the website's privacy, cookie, analytics and affiliate
disclosures, together with your browser's privacy settings. Older migrated
favorites without an article link may open a website search; the browser
then sends that search query to the website. Other linked websites have
their own privacy policies.

Contact and data requests

For privacy questions or requests concerning data associated with the app's
network requests, contact contact@kreativfont.com. To request deletion of
server-side data associated with app requests, email contact@kreativfont.com
and mention the Kreativ Font Android app. We will assess requests with the
relevant hosting provider where necessary. Retention required for
security or legal obligations may limit deletion. Do not send passwords or
other sensitive information with your request. We cannot retrieve favorites
or other information stored only on your device. Server-side request
metadata, including catalogue search phrases, may be present in hosting
logs subject to the retention described above.
