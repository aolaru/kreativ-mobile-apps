Verified app behavior

The Kreativ Font Android app displays the five latest commercial fonts and
five latest free fonts from kreativfont.com, including article descriptions
and preview images. It requests public catalogue data and images over HTTPS
from kreativfont.com and WordPress image CDN servers.

The app does not require an account and contains no advertising or analytics
SDK. It does not request access to your camera, photos, contacts or device
location. Website Google Analytics scripts are not executed inside the native
app.

Favorites, cached article details and downloaded preview images are stored
locally in the app's private storage. In-app search filters the articles already
loaded on your device. Favorites and in-app search terms are not uploaded by
the native app. You can remove individual favorites, or use Android's Clear
storage option or uninstall the app to remove its local data. Android cloud
backup and device transfer are disabled for this app.

Loading catalogue data and preview images involves network requests, which
make your IP address and request information available to the serving website
and CDN infrastructure. The actual information retained, purposes and
retention periods must be described here after verification; absence of an
analytics SDK does not imply absence of server-side collection.

Article links and the privacy policy open in your browser. Browser visits are
subject to the website's cookies, analytics and affiliate-link disclosures.
Older migrated favorites without an article link may open a website search;
in that case the browser sends the corresponding search query to the website.

Questions about the app's privacy can be sent to contact@kreativfont.com.

Outstanding checks before publication / Play Data safety submission

Review findings (11 October 2026; public appendix approved and published)

- The signed-in Cloudflare account contains three domains, but not
  kreativfont.com. Its account switcher offers no additional accounts.
  No Cloudflare settings were changed.
- Public DNS lists ns1.wordpress.com, ns2.wordpress.com and ns3.wordpress.com,
  with A records 192.0.78.224 and 192.0.78.166. The authenticated hosting
  overview subsequently confirmed "Hosted on WordPress.com" (legacy Pro plan).
- Jetpack Performance settings have Site Accelerator, image acceleration and
  static-file acceleration enabled. This matches the app's wp.com image URLs.
  Its feature-specific notice lists no visitor data use/activity tracking;
  this is not a blanket exemption for the hosting server's access logs.
  Source: https://jetpack.com/support/site-accelerator/
- Jetpack Stats is configured for the website. Google Analytics is connected
  through Site Kit, with its snippet inserted and Enhanced Measurement enabled.
  Jetpack's separate Google Analytics toggle is off; that does not mean the
  website has no Google Analytics. Native REST/image requests do not execute
  these JavaScript snippets. Browser article visits are a separate context.
- WordPress.com documents every server request in web-server logs and a
  30-day retention period. No visitor logs were exported for this review.
  Source: https://wordpress.com/support/site-logs/
- Installed plugins include Redirection. Its Options page has redirect logs
  and 404 logs set to "A day", full IP logging enabled, HTTP-header capture
  disabled and external-redirect logging disabled. Its expiry depends on
  WordPress cron; the setting is not a guaranteed immediate deletion deadline.
  This may apply to native requests that redirect or encounter a missing file.
  The plugin's optional external IP-geolocation/user-agent lookup is manual,
  not automatic, according to its documentation. It was not used in this review.
  Sources: https://redirection.me/support/options/
           https://redirection.me/support/privacy-gdpr/
- Automattic's visitor privacy notice says its website-server logs typically
  retain IP/browser/OS information for approximately 30 days for investigating
  issues. It also describes IP-derived geography for Site Stats. This is
  provider-level documentation, not proof that every listed field or Stats
  feature applies to this app's native REST/image requests.
  Source: https://automattic.com/privacy-notice/
