https://kreativfont.com/blog/terms-of-use-privacy-policy#android-app-privacy
Public Android appendix checked 18 October; effective 15 October. It distinguishes
native app behaviour from website membership, AI tools, ads and analytics.

the website. The native app contains no ad/analytics SDK, but hosting requests

to their respective creators. The app does not distribute commercial font files.
