No new app build/device test, Play mutation, purchase, website deployment, post
or email. Existing screenshots and released behaviour were checked against
2.3.0 source and the public listing. Thirteen existing locales were not rewritten.
No growth/rankings, publisher endorsement or paid placement is promised.
Browser preview status and exact static results are recorded in verification.json.
If a local-file preview is blocked, do not work around it with a different browser
or local server. Deliver local files and state which layout/playback checks remain.

alt text and captions are built in. Inspect actual rendered results where possible;
static validators do not prove screen-reader, keyboard or playback behaviour.
