# PaperBuild Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

PaperBuild is an offline Android document-maker from Kreativ. This policy explains how the app handles information when you use it.

## Our approach

PaperBuild is designed to process your document pages locally on your device. It does not require an account and does not contain advertising, analytics, tracking SDKs, or an internet permission.

## Documents and images

You choose the images that PaperBuild can access through Android's system file picker. When you choose **Scan a page**, PaperBuild opens your device's camera app to capture a page. The selected or captured images are used only to create the PDF you request.

PaperBuild does not upload your images, PDFs, document names, or page-order information to Kreativ or any other server.

## Files created by the app

While you work, PaperBuild creates a temporary PDF in its private app storage. When you choose **Share**, **Open**, or **Save to Downloads**, you control where a copy goes. Saved files are placed in `Downloads/PaperBuild`; original images are never changed.

## Data collection and sharing

PaperBuild does not collect, sell, rent, or share personal information. It does not use your documents to train AI systems.

## Permissions

On Android 8 and Android 9 only, PaperBuild may request storage permission when you choose to save a PDF to Downloads. This is required by those Android versions. On Android 10 and newer, saving uses Android's scoped storage and does not require that permission.

PaperBuild does not request broad photo-library access or direct camera permission.

## Children

PaperBuild is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to this policy

If PaperBuild's data practices change, this policy will be updated before or when the relevant app update is released. The date at the top will show when it was last changed.
