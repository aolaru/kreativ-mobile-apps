PhotoFeather Privacy Policy
Prepared 24 October 2026 · Version 1.0.0

Provider and contact
PhotoFeather is an Android photo compression and resizing app provided by Andrei Olaru, trading as Made by Kreativ, in Bucharest, Romania. This policy covers com.kreativ.photofeather. Contact info@madebykreativ.com for privacy questions or support.

Photos and on-device processing
You choose photos through Android's system picker or share them from another app. PhotoFeather accesses the items you select through the permissions Android grants for those items. It does not request broad storage or photo-library access. JPEG, PNG and static WebP inputs are supported; output is lossy JPEG.

PhotoFeather processes photos on your device. It has no internet permission, backend, account system, advertising SDK, analytics SDK or billing in version 1.0.0. It does not transmit your photos, source metadata or app activity to Made by Kreativ. Android's picker may offer cloud-provider photos; retrieving those photos is handled by Android and your chosen provider under their own settings and policies.

Local storage and retention
The app keeps imported working copies, prepared JPEGs, output settings and a recovery record in its private storage so a session can recover after a restart. Clear session removes the imported working copies and session items. Private export files that are no longer needed and are older than 24 hours are cleaned up on a later app launch; cleanup is not a background deletion service. Uninstalling the app removes its private app data under Android's normal behaviour. App-managed working data is excluded from Android backup.

Saved and shared copies
Saving creates new JPEG copies in the destination you choose or in Pictures/PhotoFeather. Originals are not overwritten or deleted. Saved copies remain outside private app storage until you delete them yourself. Sharing uses Android's share sheet: the receiving app you choose can read the shared copies and handles them under its own privacy practices. Sharing is initiated by you; PhotoFeather has no automatic uploads.

Metadata and quality
PhotoFeather applies supported image orientation to the pixels, then removes source JPEG APP and comment metadata segments from freshly encoded output. It checks those segments are absent and reopens the JPEG before export. This removes source metadata such as embedded location information from its JPEG copies; output files still have generated filenames and filesystem/gallery timestamps. It does not anonymise a photo or remove faces, text or other sensitive information visible in the pixels. JPEG output is lossy, and compression or resizing can reduce resolution or visible quality. Transparency is flattened onto the background colour you select.

Information you send for support
If you email Made by Kreativ, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use that information to respond and handle your request. Do not send sensitive photos or information unless necessary. Support correspondence is separate from on-device app processing; contact info@madebykreativ.com to ask about or request deletion of information you sent us, subject to any applicable retention obligations.

Google Play and external services
Google Play and Android may independently process downloads, installation information, diagnostics or feedback according to your settings and Google's policies. Such platform processing is separate from PhotoFeather's on-device processing. Opening an external website or contacting support uses the browser or email app you choose and those services' privacy practices.

Changes
We will update this policy if PhotoFeather's behaviour changes and identify the revision date. This policy describes version 1.0.0; it does not describe any future paid features.
