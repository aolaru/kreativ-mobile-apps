READY

REMAINING BEFORE UPLOAD / SUBMISSION
1. Sign in to Samsung Seller Portal and verify commercial seller approval.
   This account status is required even for free Android apps. No account
   registration, agreement acceptance or financial profile was completed here.
2. Confirm the production signing approach. The four PHOTOFEATHER_* signing
   variables are not configured in this session. No new key was generated.
   See SIGNING-AND-BUILD.txt. Do not upload the QA/debug APK.
3. Confirm initial launch countries, complete Samsung's age/content and privacy
   questionnaires, and review public seller contact/address information.
4. Enter the fields from listing.json and upload the icon and six screenshots.
   Samsung permits 4–8 screenshots; a Play feature graphic is not a required
   replacement for the app icon or screenshot set. No video URL is needed.
5. Upload the properly signed production APK (or complete Samsung's AAB signing
   workflow), inspect device eligibility, and perform the final portal validation.
6. Submit only when the account, signing and questionnaire gates are complete.
   This task prepares the materials; it does not authorize publishing a new app.

No runtime code or Google Play listing was changed for this preparation.
No Samsung SDK or store-specific flavor is needed for the current free app:
it does not use Google billing, Google Play services or an internet permission.
The same package is currently retained, pending the signing decision.

REGENERATE
From the PhotoFeather project, build normal production artifacts (not QA):
  JAVA_HOME='/Applications/Android Studio.app/Contents/jbr/Contents/Home' ./gradlew --offline :app:assembleRelease :app:bundleRelease
Then:
  python3 scripts/prepare-samsung.py
