Community introduction — where promotion is permitted

Disclosure: I am the developer. I made PhotoFeather, a small Android app for making photos fit upload limits. It supports file-size targets, resize presets, measured results and comparisons, with processing on the device and no ads or account. Output is lossy JPEG, so it is useful for sharing copies rather than replacing originals. I would welcome feedback on the workflow, especially awkward form limits—not a particular rating or review.
