Title: BLOCKSHIFT

Short description:
Place blocks, clear lines and reshape your board with a strategic SHIFT.

Full description:
Make a little space. BLOCKSHIFT is an offline block puzzle from Made by Kreativ.

Place three shapes on an 8 × 8 board. Clear complete rows and columns, build combinations and plan ahead to make room for your next move.

SHIFT adds another way to think. Clearing lines charges one strategic move: slide a row or column by one cell and wrap tiles around the edge. Preview your choice before applying it, or cancel to keep your charge.

FOUR WAYS TO PLAY
Classic — familiar endless block placement and a personal best to beat.
SHIFT — all the placement play, with a charged board movement when you need it.
Zen — a softer atmosphere; when no piece fits, a fresh board unfolds and your score continues.
Daily — a reproducible challenge each day with an eight-line goal and local completion history.

PLAY AT YOUR PACE
Each mode remembers its own board. Track your scores, line clears, combinations and playtime. Adjust drag height, sound effects, ambience, motion, particles, text size and haptics to make play comfortable.

This internal-test edition works offline, requires no account and includes no advertisements or purchases. Game progress is stored locally. No gameplay data is sent to Made by Kreativ.
