BLOCKSHIFT Privacy Policy

Effective 26 October 2026 · Offline internal-test edition, versions 0.5.0 and 0.5.1

Who provides the game

BLOCKSHIFT is an offline single-player Android puzzle game provided by Andrei Olaru, trading as Made by Kreativ. This policy covers package com.madebykreativ.blockshift. Contact: info@madebykreativ.com.

No collection through the game

This edition does not collect or share your gameplay data with the developer or third parties. It has no account, server connection, analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or purchase SDK. It requests no internet permission. Google Play and Android may independently process store or operating-system information under their own policies; that is not information transmitted by the game to Made by Kreativ.

Local records and retention

Scores, statistics, settings, Daily Challenge history and one saved board per game mode are retained in private storage on your device until you reset or remove them. The game uses checksummed files and recovery copies to recover interrupted writes. These records are not sent to Made by Kreativ. Android app backup is disabled in this edition. In version 0.5.1 and later, Settings → About → Back up & restore progress lets you export a portable, unencrypted file containing your local game progress and preferences. The system file picker grants access only to the file you choose; Android 10 or later is required. If you select a cloud storage provider, that provider handles the file under its own policy. The game does not upload the file to Made by Kreativ. Restoring checks the file and asks for confirmation before replacing local progress; the previous local progress is kept for Undo restore.

Removing your data

Settings → About → Privacy & local data → Reset local progress removes your scores, Daily history and saved boards while keeping preferences. Restore default settings resets preferences while keeping tutorial completion and game progress. Android's clear-app-storage controls or uninstall remove the installation's local app data. The game has no account or developer-operated cloud copy to delete. Reinstalling starts fresh unless you restore a previously exported backup. Reset local progress also removes the private Undo restore copy. Exported backup files remain in the location you chose until you delete them using that storage provider or file manager.

Permissions

The optional VIBRATE permission provides local haptic feedback. Turn it off in the game's Settings. The game does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, shared-storage or network access.

Support email and website links

If you contact us by email, we receive the information you choose to send and use it to respond and handle your request. Do not send sensitive information or payment-card details. Request deletion of support correspondence by emailing the same address; any information that must be retained for a legal obligation is handled under that obligation.

Support links open your email app. Website links open your browser. The game does not attach boards, scores, identifiers or other gameplay records to those links. Your email provider, browser and the destination website handle those interactions under their own policies. Hosting and network providers receive the technical request information needed to serve this policy page. See the Made by Kreativ website privacy policy for website processing.

Changes

This policy describes the offline internal-test edition. We will update it and the applicable store disclosures before enabling advertising, analytics or purchases. The date above identifies this policy revision.
