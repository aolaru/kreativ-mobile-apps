# Known limitations — 0.5.3

This is a signed offline internal-test candidate. It is not a completed commercial submission. Current 0.5.3 correction evidence is in review/INTERNAL_TESTING_0_5_3.md; 0.5.2 rollout/listening evidence is in review/INTERNAL_TESTING_0_5_2.md; the verified Play-installed 0.5.1 results remain in review/INTERNAL_TESTING_0_5_1.md. The active Play internal release is 0.5.3 (10), available to the two selected BLOCKSHIFT testers. The Galaxy A55 was last verified on non-debuggable Play 0.5.2; USB disconnected before 0.5.3 installation and final save comparison. Public production remains inactive.

- Native export, confirmed restore, Cancel, Undo and cold restart passed on the Galaxy A55 development installation on 7 November. The approved Play signature switch is complete, the Play tutorial passed with user-assisted touch, and the latest progress restored successfully. Play exports before and after a confirmed cold restart exactly match the original backup across all saved fields. Saved-mode cards were checked without making moves or resuming user boards. The final native export was user-assisted because mirrored file-dialog targeting was unreliable. Private backups and the original APK remain outside the repository.

- Google Play Billing and ad SDKs are disabled adapters. No real checkout, purchase restoration, entitlement verification, consent SDK, interstitial or rewarded ad has been tested. PRO cannot be purchased. Unfinished purchase and premium-theme promotions are hidden in this edition. Future additional customization is a roadmap item.
- Android build/emulator and physical Galaxy A55 checks are recorded separately in `review/VALIDATION.md`. Historical 0.4.1 Galaxy A55 checks passed migration, tutorial charging/preview/Apply, independent Classic/Zen saves and background/force-stop/resume. The current installed Play build is 0.5.2, delivered as an in-place update without uninstalling or resetting progress. The prior completed SHIFT snapshot and all high scores are preserved. Exact current scope and test-run statistics effects are recorded in review/VALIDATION.md; storage-failure injection was not performed on the user device. Broad OEM/cutout coverage, prolonged battery usage, thermal performance, accessibility services and hardware haptic/audio listening remain uncertified. The battery-saving option limits rendering to 30 fps; battery-life gains have not been measured. iOS has not been exported or tested.
- In-app 120% text scaling is implemented. Android system font-scale synchronization and a fully screen-reader-operable puzzle board are not implemented. Native menus support keyboard focus; gameplay currently requires pointing/touch input. Placement states include outlines/X marks; charge and selected-line state include text and counts.
- SFX remain original synthesized tones. Rain, Forest and Ocean are original procedural nature-style loops, not field recordings. Their decoded level/loop tests and native Settings tests pass. The user confirmed 0.5.2 playback of all three sounds and Off, then requested less Forest noise; 0.5.3 supplies that correction. Physical listening to the revised mix and hardware haptic acceptance remain pending. Final Kreativ Sound production/mastering can replace the manifest assets. Visual effects are restrained and still need longitudinal playtesting.
- The 0.3.0 presentation pass uses finite visual snapshots, original SVG materials and capped particles. The recorded preview uses controlled QA fixtures. No sustained device frame-time, battery/thermal or broad GPU certification is implied; reduced motion and battery saver remain available.
- Piece generation guarantees one legal option at each fresh deal when possible, not solvability of the entire hand. Difficulty, scoring, retention, daily objectives and ad cadence need player testing. No analytics are installed to collect this automatically.
- Dailies use device local time and a versioned seeded generator. There is no authoritative global clock, server, anti-cheat, account, cloud sync, leaderboard or localization. Replay consistency is for the same catalog/rules/Godot version; engine RNG compatibility across future upgrades is not promised.
- Saves detect accidental corruption and recover from interrupted writes. No encrypted storage or absolute power-loss durability guarantee is claimed. New future save versions are protected from overwrite; malformed interrupted runs are discarded while valid aggregate scores remain. Zen auto-renewal is immediate with a status message.
- Each mode now retains its own board. Daily has one interrupted slot, not one partial board for every date; replacing an older Daily requires confirmation. Daily history stores attempts when a run ends. Rule version 2 changes fresh Daily seeds; existing interrupted boards retain their saved RNG state and date. Completed historical runs are not revived by the new rescue rule.
- Working title BLOCKSHIFT, package identifier, icon distinctiveness and publisher rights need clearance before publication. No trademark clearance is implied.
- A dedicated upload key is stored outside the workspace and a signed internal-test bundle is prepared. Preparation scripts never upload or roll out a release. The hosted policy, 13+ audience, content rating, Data safety, public contact details and default listing/artwork are saved in Console. The latest listing and declaration changes still await submission for review. No public release is approved or implied.
