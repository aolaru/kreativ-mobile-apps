face-assist-nasa.jpg is the example group photo distributed with Google's ML Kit
face-detection guide. This is a public test fixture, not a user's private photo.
The filename in Google's documentation credits NASA. No identity recognition is
performed. Only detection bounding boxes are tested, with no stored identity data.

Guide: https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/vision/face-detection
Image: https://developers.google.com/static/ml-kit/vision/face-detection/images/1024px-Physicist_Stephen_Hawking_in_Zero_Gravity_NASA.jpg
