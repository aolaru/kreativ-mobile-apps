Community post — only where developer promotion is allowed

Developer disclosure: I made ShareSafe, an Android tool for creating photo copies with embedded metadata removed.

The useful free part: select photos, inspect selected readable EXIF fields, clean copies, then share or save JPEGs. Originals are left unchanged and photo processing is on-device.

Optional paid Pro adds manual covers and on-device face suggestions. Face detection can miss faces; metadata cleaning does not remove details visible in the image. The ML Kit SDK collects diagnostic/usage information and identifiers, not photos.

I’d appreciate feedback on whether the before/after check is understandable, especially its ‘unknown / unreadable’ results. No ratings or reviews requested.
