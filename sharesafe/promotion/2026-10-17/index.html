ShareSafe: Photo Metadata

Remove photo metadata on-device. Keep originals. Free cleaning, optional Pro.

Keep the moment. Not the metadata.

Photos can contain more than the picture: embedded location, dates and camera details. ShareSafe creates fresh JPEG copies with embedded metadata removed, while leaving your original photos unchanged. Photo processing happens on your device.

FREE PHOTO PRIVACY TOOLS
• Choose photos using Android’s system picker, without broad gallery access.
• Use Privacy Check to inspect the presence of selected readable EXIF location, date/time and camera/device fields.
• Create fresh JPEG copies and view the selected metadata checks before and after cleaning.
• Choose JPEG quality, save copies to your gallery, or share individual images or a ZIP.
• No ShareSafe account required. Metadata cleaning does not require a Pro purchase.

OPTIONAL SHARESAFE PRO — ONE-TIME PURCHASE
Advanced editing works on one photo at a time. Use solid Cover masks to hide visible details, adjust individual areas, zoom, and undo or redo edits. Blur and pixelation are also available as optional effects.

Face Assist uses an on-device model to suggest face areas. Review suggestions and apply or dismiss them yourself. It detects faces; it does not identify people, can miss faces and does not guarantee anonymity.

IMPORTANT LIMITS
Privacy Check covers selected EXIF fields, not every metadata format. Android or your photo provider may hide source metadata. Unreadable or unsupported data is unknown, not proof that a photo is safe.

Metadata cleaning does not hide faces, addresses, reflections or other details visible in the picture. Review every exported copy before sharing. Blur and pixelation can still reveal information; prefer solid Cover for stronger concealment of visible details.

Exports are JPEGs, up to 4096 pixels per side and 8 megapixels. Large photos may be resized, transparent areas become white, and animated images export one frame. Exported copies are rechecked for the selected EXIF fields; copies that fail that check are skipped.

PRIVACY
Photo processing is on-device; ShareSafe does not upload your photos or Face Assist detection results. The Play edition’s Google ML Kit SDK collects diagnostic/usage information and identifiers and may contact Google. Optional purchases use Google Play services. This is not a ‘no data collected’ claim.

Read the privacy policy: https://madebykreativ.com/privacy/sharesafe/
